Privacy

Last updated 2 October 2026

The short version

Hacking the Beats is owned and operated by Built By Signal LLC (builtbysignal.com); "we" below means that company. You can listen to and mix the featured beat without an account, and then we keep nothing about you. If you sign in with Google, we keep your name, your email address and the songs you save, so that your songs are there when you come back. We count visits with Google Analytics unless you opt out.

Without an account

A few settings (the volume, where you left the knobs and channels, which tips you have already seen, your answer about analytics) are stored in your browser on your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes them. Editing the code, saving songs, and every beat beyond the featured one need an account.

With an account

Signing in with Google gives us, through Google's Firebase service:

  • your Google account's name, email address, profile picture address and a unique account id;
  • the songs you save: their code, title, mixer settings and the times they were created and changed, and, for a copy, where it came from (the original's title, its credit, the name of whoever shared it, and its link or page).

Your songs are private to you. If you switch on sharing for a song, anyone who has that song's link can listen to it, play with its controls and keep a copy of their own, and will see your name next to it. A copy credits the song it came from, with your name, and keeps that credit after you switch sharing off. Switching sharing off makes the link stop working. The people who run the site can see the songs that are currently shared, and can switch sharing off for a song, for example after a complaint.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics, which sets cookies and tells us which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are, what kind of device they use, and when someone presses play, signs in, shares a song or keeps a copy of one, makes a recording, or starts and finishes the first steps. It is on unless you opt out. Your first visit shows a notice with an Opt out button, and you can at any time. Opting out stops the counting in that browser and removes the cookies Google Analytics set. If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, we treat that as opting out. The page that plays shared songs never loads Google Analytics.

Where it is kept

The site is served by Google Firebase Hosting, and accounts and songs are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Sound samples are fetched from the public sample packs a song names, such as GitHub; those hosts see your IP address when a sample is downloaded, as any website you visit does.

Your choices

  • You can delete any song from the songs list; it is removed from your account.
  • You can export all your songs as a file from the songs list.
  • To have your account and everything in it deleted, write to the address on the About page.
