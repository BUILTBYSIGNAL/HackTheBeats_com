              Hacking the Beats

              Every sound here is made by the code on screen, running live in your browser. Each knob is a slider() from the song, each channel is one of its labelled patterns, and two decks let you bring a second beat in over the first. The code is yours to change: click in it and type.

              Keys

              Space
              Play or stop the deck in focus
              X
              Switch focus between deck A and deck B
              M
              Mix: start the other deck in time and fade across
              ← →
              Previous or next beat
              1–9 0 - =
              Mute channels 1 to 12
              Q W E A S D
              Hold a pad: echo, drop, wash, half, stutter, no drums. With Shift it stays on.
              [ ]
              Jump back or forward four bars
              F G
              Follow the music with the code; gallery (full-screen) view
              R B
              Record the mix to a WAV file; open the beats list
              On a knob
              Arrows adjust, Shift for fine, Enter or double-click resets
              In the code
              Click a track label to mute it, click a highlighted number to find its knob, drag an inline slider
              Ctrl+.
              Stop the deck in focus (works while typing in the code, too)
              Editing
              Click in the code (or press Edit) to change it. Ctrl+Enter runs your changes without stopping the music, Ctrl+S saves, Esc leaves the code. Changed lines are marked until you save.

              MIDI controller

              Not connected.

              With Learn on, click any knob, fader, pad or button on the deck, then move a control on your hardware.
