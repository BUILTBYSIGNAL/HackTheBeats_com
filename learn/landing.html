Music you can see, change, perform and remix. Every song here is a short program written in Strudel, running live in your browser. You do not have to know how to code to start: press play, move something, hear what happens. Then find out how the music works.

Start where you are

Listener I just want to play with a beat Play, knobs, channels and pads. No account, no code. DJ I mix and perform Two decks, sync, the crossfader, pads, snapshots and a MIDI controller. Remixer I want to change a song Edit the code while it plays, rearrange the bars, keep your version. Coder I write Strudel How the site reads a song, and how to write one that performs well.
